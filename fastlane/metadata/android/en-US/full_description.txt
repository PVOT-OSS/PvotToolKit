Pvot Toolkit is the companion app for Team StormBreaker's open-source custom kernels. It shows what your device and its kernel are doing, read honestly and without root: if a value cannot be read, it is not shown.

Your device at a glance
• Model, Android version and kernel release, and whether it is a StormBreaker kernel
• Battery, memory, CPU frequency and governor in one place
• The latest news from the StormBreaker project

Live metrics
• Overall CPU load and a chart for every core, each with its own frequency and cluster
• CPU clusters read per frequency policy, with their peak clocks and governors
• Memory, battery level, temperature and current, network traffic and storage
• Uptime with how much of it the device spent in deep sleep
• GPU identification and the framework's thermal throttling status

Hardware and build details
• System, processor, screen, memory and battery
• Every camera: resolution, aperture, focal length, sensor and pixel size, ISO range, zoom and hardware level
• Media codecs, and boot and security state (bootloader lock, verified boot, encryption)
• Every sensor on the device, with its vendor part names

Battery monitor
• Optional and off by default: tracks active and idle drain in an ongoing notification, from the moment you turn it on

News and community
• Kernel releases, project updates and announcements from Team StormBreaker
• Links to the community, the source code and the issue tracker

The Tune and Builds tabs are placeholders for kernel controls and kernel updates in later releases.

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking
• Everything the app reads about your device stays on your device; it only downloads the project's news and links
• Free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/PvotToolKit
